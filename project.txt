WHAT IT IS  (v2 framing — supersedes the homeschool-only v1)
A group of North Coast families raising children for the world ahead. NOT homeschool-only —
school families, home-educating families and everything between are all welcome. Two levels:

  LEVEL 1 — THE DAYS OUT (running now, easy to join, small cost)
    Regular days out together: parks, hired-coach trips, beaches, forests, workshops.
    Weekly, vs the once-a-month most groups manage. Fits around school = weekends/holidays.
    This is the front door and the actual CTA. A small group of families is ALREADY doing this.

  LEVEL 2 — THE FACILITY (in preparation, selective, much larger monthly cost)
    Permanent place on the North Coast: barn/indoor park, garden, quiet house, work rooms
    + a parent work zone. 8am-8pm, SEVEN days. Parents stay on site and stay responsible.
    Places go to families already known from the days out.

Ages 3-8 for both, siblings a year either side welcome (so a 4 + an 8 can come together).

CORE MESSAGE
"Raising our children for the world ahead — not the one that has gone."
The family is the centre; everything else is supplemental. Open to the best way — we take
what holds up from Montessori, Waldorf, forest school and the classical tradition, and drop
what does not. Preparing children for a world of machines that recall everything, so the
weight goes on figuring it out, getting on with people, and finishing what you started.

you decide -> facility place later for families we know.

THE APPLICATION — apply.html (this is a designed experience, do not flatten it into a form)
Nine screens, one question each, ~3 minutes. Every CTA on index.html points here.
Placeholder key: YOUR_WEB3FORMS_ACCESS_KEY (once, top of the script). Guard: while it is a
placeholder the button says "Email it to school@aeonreon.com" instead of erroring.
One Web3Forms POST at the very end with every answer. localStorage keeps a half-finished
application alive (key: sol-application-v1), cleared on success.

  0  The open        Tone + permission to leave + first small yes ("I would like to be considered")
  1  Children's ages Tap chips. Zero friction, and it is about their child not about them
  2  School status   Four options, ALL valid. A mirror, not a filter - teaches that the group is broad
  3  Principle: tasks not tests   They read it, then react. Endorsing it is a small commitment
  4  Principle: you stay          The real filter. "What I need is childcare" -> the exit screen
  5  "What do you want your child to be like at eighteen?"  The emotional core. Gated at 5+ words
  6  What could you bring         Flips them from customer to member. Chips + optional text
  7  Details                      Name/email/town/phone/days. LAST, when they are already invested
  8  Done                         Peak-end: their own words played back, what happens next, the five
                                  things we believe, one thing to try this week, links to the apps
  9  The exit                     Warm, honest, hands them Days Out NI + Conscious Parenting instead

WHY IT IS BUILT THIS WAY (keep these if the page is ever rewritten)
- Self-persuasion: they state the philosophy in their own words, so they own it
- Effort justification: the more they put in, the more the place is worth to them
- Progressive commitment: micro-yeses first, personal data last
- Real turn-away: the exit screen is what makes acceptance mean something
- Peak-end: the last screen is the emotional high, not a receipt
- Honest progress bar: the open, the finish and the exit are excluded from it
