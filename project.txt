WHAT IT IS
A home-education community on the North Coast of Northern Ireland. A facility — barn,
garden, quiet house, work rooms — where children aged 3-7 play, build and learn in a
group, AND their parents stay on site. Parents work in a dedicated work zone, remain
legally responsible for their own child, and help shape what the children do.
Open 8am-8pm, six days a week. Families come and go within that window.

Public framing: "The School of Light — a home-education community on the North Coast."
NOT a registered school. Parents elect home education; we provide place, group, programme.
