WHAT IT IS  (v2 framing — supersedes the homeschool-only v1)
A group of North Coast families raising children for the world ahead. NOT homeschool-only —
school families, home-educating families and everything between are all welcome. Two levels:

  LEVEL 1 — THE DAYS OUT (running now, easy to join, small cost)
    Regular days out together: parks, hired-coach trips, beaches, forests, workshops.
    Weekly, vs the once-a-month most groups manage. Fits around school = weekends/holidays.
    This is the front door and the actual CTA. A small group of families is ALREADY doing this.

  LEVEL 2 — THE FACILITY (in preparation, selective, much larger monthly cost)
    Permanent place on the North Coast: barn/indoor park, garden, quiet house, work rooms
    + a parent work zone. 8am-8pm, SEVEN days. Parents stay on site and stay responsible.
    Places go to families already known from the days out.

Ages 3-8 for both, siblings a year either side welcome (so a 4 + an 8 can come together).

CORE MESSAGE
"Raising our children for the world ahead — not the one that has gone."
The family is the centre; everything else is supplemental. Open to the best way — we take
what holds up from Montessori, Waldorf, forest school and the classical tradition, and drop
what does not. Preparing children for a world of machines that recall everything, so the
weight goes on figuring it out, getting on with people, and finishing what you started.

TWO APPLICATIONS — THIS IS THE ARCHITECTURE, KEEP IT
  1. THE DAY OUT (built) — apply.html. Friendly, simple, low pressure. A day out is a
     low-risk yes, so the application is an invitation, not an interview.
  2. THE FACILITY (NOT BUILT — deliberately) — a separate, longer, more serious process,
     to be designed carefully later. It happens with families we already know from the
     days out. The site says so; do not merge it back into apply.html.

apply.html — 7 screens, ~2 minutes. Every CTA on index.html points here.
Key: YOUR_WEB3FORMS_ACCESS_KEY (once, top of script). Guard: while it is a placeholder the
button says "Email it to school@aeonreon.com". One Web3Forms POST at the end.
localStorage key sol-dayout-application-v1, cleared on success.

  0  The open        "This is the easy bit." Names the first-day awkwardness out loud
  1  Children's ages Chips. One tap, and it is about their child not about them
  2  What sort of day would you enjoy   Fun, low stakes, makes them picture it, real planning data
  3  How our days feel  THE CULTURE FILTER. Good bit first, then What everybody brings /
                        What we leave at home, then "this is not a purity test" + explicit
                        permission to work (bring your laptop, take the call)
  4  What made you look at this   One light open question, NOT gated
  5  Details         Name/email/town/phone/days. Last, once they have pictured the day
  6  Done            Peak-end: The Thinking PDF download, what happens next, the five
                     beliefs, one thing to try this week, links to Days Out NI + the app

CULTURE CODE (the wording matters — it is the whole filter)
  Brings: good energy + a hand / curiosity both ways / kindness to every child not only
    your own / food you would be glad for any child to see you eating / your own mess home
  Leaves at home: smoking or vaping near children / fizzy drinks, sweets, the beige-and-
    orange end of the menu / talking down to children / turning up to be entertained
  Then: NOT a purity test, nobody keeping score, the standard is our own example because
    at 3-8 children copy what they see. And: you ARE allowed to work.

THE THINKING — the-thinking.html -> the-school-of-light.pdf (9 pages, A4)
Handed over on the day-out finish screen. Sections: why this exists / what we actually are /

REGENERATE after editing the HTML (playwright lives in browser-engine, so run it from there):
  cd APPS/browser-engine && cat > sol-pdf.mjs <<'JS'
  import { chromium } from 'playwright';
  const dir = '/Users/aiautomator/Documents/Documents/WEBSITES/PROJECTS/TO BE BUILT/SchoolOfLight/';
  const b = await chromium.launch();
  const p = await b.newPage({ viewport: { width: 794, height: 1123 } });
  await p.goto('file://' + encodeURI(dir + 'the-thinking.html'), { waitUntil: 'networkidle' });
  await p.emulateMedia({ media: 'print' });
  await p.pdf({ path: dir + 'the-school-of-light.pdf', format: 'A4', printBackground: true,
    margin: { top: '18mm', bottom: '16mm', left: '18mm', right: '18mm' } });
  await b.close();
  JS
  node sol-pdf.mjs && rm sol-pdf.mjs
